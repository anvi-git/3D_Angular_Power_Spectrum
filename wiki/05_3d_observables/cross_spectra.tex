\subsection{Cross-Spectra Between Observables}\label{subsec:obs_cross_spectra}

This section discusses cross-correlations between different tracers (convergence × convergence, convergence × clustering, etc.).

\subsubsection{Auto vs cross-spectra}

- **Auto-spectrum** ($i = j$): $C_\ell^{ii} = C_\ell^{AA \times AA}$ (self-correlation)
- **Cross-spectrum** ($i \neq j$): $C_\ell^{ij}$ (correlation between different tracers or bins)

\subsubsection{Convergence × convergence}

Angular power spectrum of weak lensing convergence:

\begin{equation}
C_\ell^{\kappa\kappa} = \int_0^\infty \frac{dk}{2\pi^2} k^2 P(k) \left[\int d\chi \, W^\kappa(\chi) j_\ell(k\chi)\right]^2
\end{equation}

Measured by galaxy shape distortions (cosmic shear).

\subsubsection{Clustering × clustering}

Angular power spectrum of galaxy positions:

\begin{equation}
C_\ell^{\delta\delta} = \int_0^\infty \frac{dk}{2\pi^2} k^2 P(k) \left[\int d\chi \, W^\delta(\chi) j_\ell(k\chi)\right]^2
\end{equation}

where $W^\delta(\chi) = n(\chi) b(\chi) D_+(\chi)$.

Measured by galaxy clustering (photometric or spectroscopic).

\subsubsection{Convergence × clustering (cross-signal)}

Cross-correlation between lensing convergence and galaxy clustering:

\begin{equation}
C_\ell^{\kappa\delta} = \int_0^\infty \frac{dk}{2\pi^2} k^2 P(k) \left[\int d\chi \, W^\kappa(\chi) j_\ell(k\chi)\right] \left[\int d\chi \, W^\delta(\chi) j_\ell(k\chi)\right]
\end{equation}

This cross-signal is powerful for cosmology because:
- Independent source of 3D information
- Cross-covariance with auto-spectra is typically small
- Breaks degeneracies in bias and growth

\remark{
Cross-correlations are often cleaner than individual auto-spectra because many systematics (shot noise, PSF effects) don't correlate between independent measurements.
}

See \cref{subsec:obs_redshift_binning} for how bins combine in cross-spectra.
