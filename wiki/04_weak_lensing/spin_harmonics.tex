\subsection{Spin-Weight Spherical Harmonics}\label{subsec:wl_spin_harmonics}

This section introduces spin-weighted harmonics needed to decompose tensor fields like shear.

\subsubsection{Spin weight concept}

A quantity with spin weight $s$ transforms as $e^{is\alpha}$ under a rotation by angle $\alpha$ about the radial axis.

- Scalars (convergence): $s = 0$ → use standard $Y_{\ell m}$
- Shear (tidal distortion): $s = \pm 2$ → use ${}_{\pm 2}Y_{\ell m}$

\subsubsection{E/B decomposition}

Shear expands as:

\begin{equation}
\gamma = \sum_{\ell m} \left[\gamma^E_{\ell m} {}_{-2}Y_{\ell m} + \gamma^B_{\ell m} {}_{+2}Y_{\ell m}\right]
\end{equation}

**E-modes** ($\gamma^E$): Curl-free; sourced by lensing potential  
**B-modes** ($\gamma^B$): Curl-like; zero for pure gravity (systematics indicator)

\subsubsection{Harmonics from lensing potential}

In harmonic space:

\begin{equation}
\kappa_{\ell m} = \frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2} \psi_{\ell m}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\gamma^E_{\ell m} = \frac{(\ell-1)(\ell+2)}{2} \psi_{\ell m}
\end{equation}

These algebraic relations follow from Laplacian (→ $\ell(\ell+1)$) and higher derivatives on the sphere.

See \cref{subsec:wl_convergence_shear} for definitions of $\kappa$ and $\gamma$.
